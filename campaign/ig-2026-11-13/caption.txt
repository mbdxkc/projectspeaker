CAPTION

Contact your rep before Nov. 18.

House Democrats pick their nominee for speaker by secret ballot on Nov. 18, according to ABC News and Bloomberg Government. Your representative casts one of those ballots.

Project Speaker makes the call easy. Tap "Find my representative" at projectspeaker.org, share your location once and the button becomes "Call Rep." plus your representative's name. It dials the Washington office and gives you the words: you want a contested election for speaker.

Then sign the petition. More than 1.17 million people have.
