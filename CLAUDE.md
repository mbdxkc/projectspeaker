- **The parade (Nov. 7, many rounds of Valdez's notes; current state):** `band.js` draws a marching band and a crowd of pro-democracy protesters behind the header text at .5 opacity, once per load at a random 3-20 s after `load` unless paused, and again whenever Play is pressed. Each pass deals a fresh scene (`build()` calls `scene()` every time)
  - **Band:** trained but not perfect. Rows 58 units apart; drum major (sometimes fumbles the baton), majorette, flag, three trumpets (lift their horns now and then), solo snare, sometimes the serious trombonist (40%; sunglasses, no bounce, slide on the beat), two sousaphones, a big drumline (five snares, two tenors, five graded bass drums, two cymbals), trumpet, two folding-chair dancers, smoke. Drift ±7 units and tempo within 5% (`pace(7, who, 0.05)`); up to one straggler, one or two trips, sometimes one marcher turns the wrong way for a beat (`b-oops`)
  - **Crowd:** a dim back row, a front row among the musicians and a trail of 620-900 units that thins into stragglers. Each person does one thing on their own clock (`clock()`): sign up and down, fist pumps, flag, wave, clap, walk, hand drum, tambourine, horn; random height, depth and size; drift ±34 (stragglers ±50) and tempo ±15%. No protest signs were removed: "lose the sign" meant the song
  - **Banners (Nov. 7):** cheerleaders lead: three carry "TELL ME WHAT DEMOCRACY LOOKS LIKE" (ivory cloth, navy text) on poles held off the shoulder, five tumblers ahead cartwheel, back-flip and toe-touch with pom-poms. Ordinary citizens bring up the rear with "THIS IS WHAT DEMOCRACY LOOKS LIKE!" (blue cloth, white text). Each banner is drawn once in `<defs>` and shown through 14 nested-`<svg>` slices that bob a beat apart (`banner()`), so it ripples with the words intact. English on both pages: it is the chant
  - **Never put a `transform` attribute and an animated `b-` class on the same element:** the CSS animation's transform replaces the attribute, and the element jumps to the figure's origin. Position on a wrapper, animate the child (`pompom()`, `spark()`, `note()`). Sparkles and notes had this bug from the first band until Nov. 7
  - **Tempo:** 96 bpm, an easy Sunday parade. Crossing ~50 px/s, rounded to whole 2.5 s bars: 45 s at 390, 60 s at 1280. Every rhythm in CSS sits on the .625 s beat and scales by each figure's `--t`
  - **Sound: none** (Valdez, Nov. 7, "lose all the sound"). `CONFIG.drums`, `horns` and `chant` are all false, so `startMusic()` returns before an AudioContext is ever created. The synth, the score and the unlock-on-first-tap logic stay in `band.js`; setting a flag to true brings that part back
- **Play/Pause** (`#pref-play`, right of "Sign the petition", both pages; replaced the Sound and Motion toggles): Play while nothing moves, Pause while the parade crosses; labels from `data-play`/`data-pause` ("Reproducir/Pausar el desfile"). Pause stops parade and sound, sets `motion-off` (rotor holds) and stores `ps-parade=off`, so no automatic parade next visit; Play sends a new one and stores `on`. Reduced motion counts as paused when nothing is stored. `apply()` must run before `parade()`: a paused band is `display: none` and measures 0 wide
